# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in SmartTools welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before making assumptions.
- Give and gracefully receive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, publication of private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, restrict participation, or take other action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method in `SECURITY.md`. Please do not make public reports containing sensitive details.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the SmartTools project or its community.
